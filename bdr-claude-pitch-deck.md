# BDR + Claude AI Pitch Deck — Complete Content Package

## Presentation Brief

- **Audience:** VP of Business Development Representatives (BDRs)
- **Company type:** IT research and advisory services
- **Team size:** 40+ BDRs
- **Current tools:** Salesforce + SalesLoft
- **Format:** 12-slide presentation (16:9 widescreen)
- **Tone:** Professional but energizing. Not overly technical. Data-driven but easy to follow.
- **Story arc:** Problem → Solution → Proof → Impact → Ask
- **Design guidance:** Use bold stat callouts, before/after comparisons, clean icon grids, and dramatic bar charts. Minimal text per slide — bullet points, not paragraphs. Modern, corporate aesthetic.
