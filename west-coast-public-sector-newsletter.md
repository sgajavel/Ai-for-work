## 🎙️ Podcast Spotlight: Digital Disruption

Info-Tech's *Digital Disruption* podcast (hosted by Geoff Nielson) just picked up a **Webby Honoree** nod in the 30th Annual Webby Awards — placing it in the top 25% of all entries submitted this year. If it's not in your rotation yet, here's where to start:

- **"How AI Will Change Society Forever"** — former OpenAI Head of Go-to-Market Zack Kass on what AI adoption actually looks like at scale, and what it means for public institutions
- **"A Worm Is Changing the Future of AI"** — Ramin Hasani (CEO, Liquid AI; MIT CSAIL) breaks down liquid neural networks in terms any non-data-scientist can follow
- **AI Is Reshaping Work, But Leadership Mindsets Must Catch Up** — Ian Beacraft on the leadership and culture shifts that have to accompany any AI rollout
- **Making Creativity a Business Strategy** — former Disney exec Duncan Wardle on building innovation capacity into risk-averse organizations (a familiar challenge in government)

👉 [Listen on the Digital Disruption hub](https://www.infotech.com/digital-disruption) · also on YouTube, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify
