# Meetings

Notes, transcripts, and content from internal meetings: enablement, training,
territory kickoffs, manager one-on-ones, and product updates.

This is **internal** content. Discovery calls and prospect-facing meeting prep do not
live here, they belong with the account in `accounts/`.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`, for example `2026-08-13-territory-kickoff.md`.

## What to capture

When Sairam drops raw notes or a transcript, the useful output is not a summary of
what was said. It is what changes:

- **Decisions** that change how the territory gets worked
- **Rules** that should be promoted into `CLAUDE.md` or the playbook
- **Names, ownership, and routing** that affect the scope check
- **Dates and deadlines**
- **Open questions** to take back

Anything that becomes a standing rule gets written into `CLAUDE.md` or
`playbook/outreach-playbook.md` and referenced from here, rather than living only in
a meeting note. Standing rules that sit buried in a dated file stop getting followed.
