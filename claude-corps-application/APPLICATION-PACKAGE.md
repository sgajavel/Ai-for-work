Before you submit, confirm these two requirements. They are the only things standing
between you and a genuinely strong application:

1. **US work authorization.** Claude Corps requires US work authorization that does

   OPT/STEM OPT and H-1B1). Your resume says "TN visa eligible" — if TN eligibility is
   your only path to US work authorization, you are **not currently eligible** and
   should not attest otherwise on the form. If you hold a green card, US citizenship,

   in **October 2024**, which puts you at ~22 months today. You cross the two-year line
   in **October 2026**. If you're applying, apply **now**, and keep resume language

   from the v2.0 resume for this application).
