## Holdover rules on open opportunities

Two hold rules protect continuity during the transition.

**1. Accounts transitioning to other Acquire reps**

Reps keep all accounts with an **FAC in the last 24 months**. Opportunities must move
to **QO by January** to retain ownership for the full fiscal. If they do not, the
account moves.

> Source inconsistency: the body slide says accounts move **February 1, 2027**, the
> FAQ says **January 1, 2027**. Confirm which is correct before relying on the date.

**2. Accounts transitioning out of or into Acquire**

Held with the current owner until **October 1** only if all three are true:

- Opportunity stage is at Proposal or beyond
- CPQ has been sent to the contact
- Meaningful talk time and outreach within the previous 60 days

**Contested FACs:** where two reps have an FAC on the same account, the **most recent
qualified FAC wins**.

*Exception, Micro NA and LATAM: all reps get 6 months to move open opps to QO.*

## Territory review process

The window is open now and closes August 19. This is a **controlled data correction
process, not an account fight.**

**Flow:** Rep submits → Director triages → VP approves → Territory Team reviews.
If a request is not objective, material, and VP-approved, it does not reach the
Territory Team.

### Eligible

- Holdover opportunity was not accurately reflected
- Sector classification is incorrect
- Account size classification is incorrect
- Buying-center grouping is wrong or unnecessary
- A Salesforce data issue materially affected assignment

### Not eligible

- Preference or tenure alone ("I've always owned it")
- Speculative future deals without supporting evidence
- Accounts below the holdover threshold
- Anything not yet validated with Director and VP

### Required on every submission

Account Name · SFDC link · LinkedIn · Current owner · Future owner · Category ·
Rationale in 3-5 bullets of facts, evidence, and business impact.

Default bar is a proposal with CPQ. Close-by expectation is October 1.

> "Escalation is a privilege of evidence, not volume."

## Account grouping, and what it means for the conglomerate test

D&B ultimate-parent grouping produced account groups too large to work, which buried
hundreds of buying centers. F27 uses Clay/AI to subdivide them.

**An F27 "group" is a cluster of entities sharing a real decision-maker, brand,
leadership, and P&L, with one procurement process, sized so one rep can own it.**

Structure: `[Ultimate Parent] | [Operating Group] | [Region]`

The FAQ states the rule directly:

> "An SMB-sized account that is part of a larger enterprise buying group stays with
> the LE territory that owns that group."

**This refines the conglomerate test.** The question is not only "does it have a
parent." It is **"does it share a decision-making unit with a larger group."** An
entity with a distant parent but its own decision maker, brand, leadership, P&L, and
procurement process is its own group, and can be in the book.

### Other reasons an account may have moved

2. Accounts once thought unrelated, now proven related, grouped together

5. EU accounts held in North America moved to the EU team

## Why the change happened

The org-level equation:

```
Revenue = FACs x Win Rate x ASP
```

Between 2020 and 2025: FACs per rep roughly flat (64.9 → 66.1), **win rate more than
halved (10.1% → 5.7%)**, ASP up **3.8x** ($15.5K → $58.9K). Revenue growth came
almost entirely from deal size while conversion fell.

The diagnosis is that buying changed. In FY20 it was CIO-led, smaller deals, light
procurement, relationship-driven. In FY26 it is CFO/CEO/board influenced, larger
deals, formal procurement with RFPs and security and legal review, and business-case
driven. More stakeholders, higher scrutiny, greater risk aversion.

The response is specialization: three segments matched to three buying motions, on
the argument that expertise compounds through repetition in a way training alone
does not.

**For the SMB motion specifically, the named focus area is meeting generation and
qualification.** That is the lever, and it maps directly onto the fast start plan.
