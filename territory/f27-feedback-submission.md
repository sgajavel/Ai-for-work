## The argument these submissions rest on

All eight items below are **holdover releases**, category: *holdover opp not reflected*.

The F27 rule for an account moving to another Acquire rep is that the current rep keeps
it where there is an FAC in the last 24 months, **and the opportunity must reach QO by
January or the account moves**.

Where there is no opportunity at all, or the opportunity is closed lost with no live
communication, **the QO condition cannot be satisfied**. The hold has no path to the
outcome it exists to protect. That is an objective, material data point, not a
preference, and it is the frame every item below is written in.

Nineteen accounts in the proposed book are held to Feb 1, 2027. These eight are the ones
where the condition is unmeetable.

## Items pulled from the submission, and why

Two of the accounts flagged in review should **not** go in as holdover releases. Both
have a bigger problem than their move date, and submitting them as acceleration
requests would work against the stronger argument.

### Vodacom — there are two records, and the book should not hold either

The export carries **two Vodacom records**:

| Record | Viability | Owner | Moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vodacom (001a700000L8URBAA3) | Viable | Sean Burkett | **August 24, 2026** |
| VODACOM GROUP LTD (0014y00002lttiaAAA) | Non-Viable | Andrew Daigle | Feb 1, 2027 |

So the viable record already moves on August 24. The one held to February is the
non-viable duplicate.

More to the point, both bill to **Gauteng, South Africa** and both sit in the
**Vodafone Group Plc** account group under `Vodacom & Africa OpCo | Southern Africa`.
That is an enterprise buying group on another continent. It does not belong in a
California SMB book at all.

**Recommendation:** submit Vodacom as a *territory assignment error*, not a holdover
release, and ask for both records to come out of the book. Asking to receive it sooner
and asking to have it removed are contradictory submissions.

### KnowledgeStorm — non-viable record inside an enterprise media group

- Non-Viable, billing state **Georgia**, ITRG status Winback.
- Account group is **Informa PLC**, specifically `Informa TechTarget (B2B Tech Media,
  Research & Data Services)`. Informa is a FTSE 100 company.
- Two viable siblings in the same group, NetLine and BrightTALK, are also in the book.

Accelerating a non-viable Georgia record inside a FTSE 100 buying group invites the
reviewer to look at the whole Informa cluster, which is more likely to end with all
three leaving the book than with KnowledgeStorm arriving early.

**Recommendation:** leave it out of this submission. If the Informa group is worth
contesting, that is a buying-group question to raise separately once the group has been
mapped.

## Reconciling this with the export

The territory export reads `Open Opportunities Proposal+ = 0` on all 699 rows, which
looked at first like there is no opportunity history anywhere in the book. That column
counts only **open** opportunities at Proposal stage or beyond. A closed lost cycle, or
a cycle that died before Proposal, does not appear in it.

Sairam's account-by-account review is therefore adding information the export cannot
carry, and it strengthens rather than contradicts the picture: the accounts held to
February are held on FAC recency, and the opportunities behind those holds are either
absent or dead.
